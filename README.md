## 🇨🇳 中文

由 2 个技能组成的学术调研与提案套件，解决选题依据从哪来、立项书怎么写这两件事。academic-researcher 面向尚未锁定具体题目的研究者、学生和论文写作者，对某个方向、概念、机制、热点前沿或学术史做系统检索：核验引用真实性、给证据分级、做主题聚类与交叉综合，识别争议与研究空白，产出结论先行、引用可追溯的结构化调研结果，并支持找文献支撑与选题依据。research-proposal 面向国自然、国社科等基金申请、开题报告、博士后与人才计划、研究计划书，负责撰写、审查和优化，把已有研究成果组织成立项书，区分事实与 AI 建议，检查任务书一致性并补强论证逻辑。两者配合使用：调研结论做选题依据，提案技能把它变成可提交的本子。

> ⚠️ **这是一套配合使用的技能组合（2 个）** —— 正文互相引用，建议成套取用。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 系统检索：围绕研究方向、概念、机制、热点前沿与学术史成体系地找文献
- 证据可信：引用真实性核验、证据分级，结论先行且每条引用可追溯
- 综合输出：主题聚类、交叉综合，明确争议点、研究空白与最新进展
- 提案撰写：国自然/国社科基金申请、开题报告、博士后与人才计划、研究计划书
- 提案审查：区分事实与 AI 建议、检查任务书一致性、补强论证逻辑
- 分工明确：调研只做检索与证据支撑，不代写论文段落；提案负责成文与优化

## 🇬🇧 English

Two skills for academic research and proposals, answering where a topic's justification comes from and how to write the proposal itself. academic-researcher targets researchers, students and paper writers who have not yet fixed a title: it systematically searches a direction, concept, mechanism, hot frontier or scholarly history, verifies citation authenticity, grades evidence, clusters themes, cross-synthesizes, identifies controversies and gaps, and produces structured findings that lead with conclusions and keep every citation traceable. research-proposal covers grant applications, proposal defenses, postdoc and talent programs and research plans, writing, reviewing and optimizing them by turning existing results into a proposal, separating facts from AI suggestions, checking consistency with the task book and strengthening the argument chain. Used together, the research findings become the justification and the proposal skill turns them into a submittable document.

> ⚠️ **A cooperating suite of 2 skills** — they reference each other; use them as a group.

### ✨ What it does

- Systematic search: find literature across a direction, concept, mechanism, hot frontier or scholarly history in a structured way
- Verifiable evidence: check citation authenticity, grade evidence, lead with conclusions and keep every citation traceable
- Synthesis: cluster themes, cross-synthesize, and surface controversies, research gaps and the latest progress
- Proposal drafting: grant applications, proposal defenses, postdoc and talent programs, and research plans
- Proposal review: separate facts from AI suggestions, check consistency with the task book and strengthen the argument chain
- Clear division: research does search and evidence only and never writes paper sections, while the proposal skill owns the finished document
